The explanatory medium

Awaiting operator ruling brief 2026-07-26 · ballot 2026-07-27 4 open — 1 gates the other 3 · 2 settled

Wayfinder plans big work as decision tickets and posts each resolution as a comment on the tracker. Briefs have outgrown that box: a brief this size gets scanned, not read, and the one part that needs John — the ask — sits below several hundred lines of argument. This ticket decides the medium: whether a decision brief gets a designed shape that puts the ask above the argument, or stays as it is.
